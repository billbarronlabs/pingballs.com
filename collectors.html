George

State College, PA • eBay: pingballs1

Started collecting Ping golf balls in September 2001. Has approximately 140 different 2-color combinations and displays around 400 Ping golf balls. His objective is to collect an Eye and Eye2 ball of every 2-color combination Ping produced. He occasionally buys, sells and trades balls with other collectors. This hobby has been a great way to have fun and to develop new friendships.

Lynne

Celina, Ohio • eBay: pingdom

A teacher from Celina, Ohio. The Ping ball collection was the idea of their daughter, who was eight years old when she decided to start collecting Ping golf balls. Her small collection grew into a family hobby, so they buy and sell Pings on eBay to both enhance their collection and to introduce new collectors to the hobby.
