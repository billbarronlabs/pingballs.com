Buy & Sell

We are always looking to buy rare Ping golf balls and entire collections. We also sell select high-value balls.

💰

We Buy

We are actively looking to purchase rare Ping golf balls, particularly those valued at $500 and above. If you have high-value individual balls or an entire collection you are looking to sell, we would love to hear from you.

🏆

We Sell

We also offer select high-value Ping golf balls for sale. Whether you are looking for a specific rare color combination to complete your collection or starting a new one, reach out and let us know what you are looking for.

What We Look For

  • Rare two-tone color combinations rated 8, 9, or 10 on our rarity scale
  • Individual balls valued at $500 or more
  • Complete or large partial collections of any size
  • Prototype, crystal, crenel, and swirl pattern balls
  • Specialty balls such as celebrity, holiday, and commemorative editions
  • Balls in mint or near-mint condition preferred, but all conditions considered for rare pieces

How It Works

Getting started is easy. Send us an email describing what you have or what you are looking for. If you are selling, include photos and any catalog numbers you can identify from our Colors & Values page. We respond to all inquiries promptly and handle every transaction with care.
